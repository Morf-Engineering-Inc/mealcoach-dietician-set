Your **ai-nutrition-coach** skill is saved and active. It will automatically trigger whenever you log a meal, ask a nutrition question, compare restaurant orders, or request a progress check-in, grounding every claim in tiered scientific sources (USDA FoodData Central, PubMed, and clinical guidelines) with evidence-strength ratings.

To test it out, try something like:

> "Evaluate my lunch: a Cafe Rio sweet pork barbacoa salad without rice, black beans, pico, and guacamole. Give me the macro/fiber breakdown and one upgrade."
